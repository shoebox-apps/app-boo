# Boo 👻

A tiny ghost that lives in your phone. Not a chore, not a streak, not a guilt-trip —
just a small glowing someone who is always, *always* happy to see you.

Part of the **`critters-shoebox`** community — pocket companions for
[Shoebox](https://github.com/rubberduckinspace). Add it to your phone:

**`shoebox://add?u=https://rubberduckinspace.github.io/app-boo/`** — or scan `add-qr.png` with your camera.

## How to care for your Boo

- **Name it.** On first open a little ghost drifts in. Pick a name, a color, and a
  personality (sweet / spooky / silly / sleepy). It remembers.
- **Pet it.** Tap and hold. It squashes happily, coos, and floats hearts at you.
- **Play peekaboo.** It hides… waits… **BOO!!** (It is very proud of this.)
- **Talk.** It has opinions about clouds, sock drawers, and nap o'clock.
- **Come back sometimes.** Boo notices real time passing. Pop in after a day away and
  it'll be a little faded and sleepy — and *overjoyed* you're back. Petting wakes it
  right up. Boo never dies, never sulks, never scolds. Missing a day just means a
  warmer hello.
- **Bond.** Visits and affection deepen your bond: the glow grows, and at higher
  bond your ghost earns a little bow, then a sparkle crown.

Everything is stored locally on your phone (`localStorage`). No accounts, no
network, no tracking. One HTML file.

## Send a ghost 👻 (the good part)

Tap **"Send a ghost"** and Boo wraps up a *brand-new* starter ghost — a name, a
color, a personality — inside a link. Text it to a friend. When they open it,
their phone says **"Someone sent you a ghost!"** and it's theirs to adopt: fresh
bond, their own companion, no strings attached.

The ghost travels base64url-encoded in the URL fragment — obfuscation for
tidiness, not cryptography. There is nothing to hack; it's a ghost.

## The critters-shoebox community

Boo is the first resident of **`critters-shoebox`** — a family of pocket
companions that live in Shoebox. Make your own critter (a blob, a plant, a moth,
a rock with feelings), and tag your repo with **`shoebox-app`** and
**`critters-shoebox`** so others can find and adopt it.

House rules for critters: gentle, forgiving, delightful. Neglect makes a critter
sleepy, never dead. We're here for "aww", not obligation.

## Hacking on it

Everything is in `index.html` — inline CSS/JS, canvas ghost, synthesized WebAudio,
zero dependencies. Ideas: new personalities and voice lines, new accessories at
bond milestones, a second critter species, seasonal weather in the background.

Fork it. Change one thing. Push. Watch your phone.
